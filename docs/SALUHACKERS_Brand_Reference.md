## 1 · Company

Online **AI school exclusively for healthcare professionals**. It does not teach AI in the abstract — it teaches clinicians the **methodologies, tools and frameworks** to fold AI into real clinical workflows, so the bureaucratic parts of the job shrink and they can *re-enamorarse de la profesión*.

*   **Mission:** translate every AI tool into **real day-to-day clinical use cases** — reducing documentation load, analyzing clinical literature, building personal knowledge bases, shipping bespoke solutions — without needing to code or spend big money.
*   **The "FOMO filter":** clinicians have no time to keep up with practical AI training; the brand curates and filters the noise for them.
*   **Empowerment framing:** by building solutions themselves, learners gain durable skills for an uncertain technological *and* healthcare future — *"que da miedo e ilusiona a partes iguales."*
*   **Course levels:** iniciados · amateurs · pros — every course is tagged so students self-select.

### Two surfaces, one visual style
*   **B2B** — through institutions: colegios médicos, pharmaceutical companies.
*   **B2C** — direct through **Academia SALUHACKERS**.

> **Value proposition.** No enseñamos IA. Enseñamos a los sanitarios metodologías, herramientas y marcos de trabajo para aplicar en sus flujos de trabajo — para reducir las partes más aburridas de su trabajo, re-enamorarse de la profesión, y convertirse en mejores profesionales. *Cada curso responde a un problema real del entorno clínico, y la herramienta es el medio, no el fin.*

> ⚠ **Naming open question.** The poster and project say **SALUHACKERS**, but the territory doc's body calls the brand **"AgorIA"** throughout ("territorio AgorIA", "Detrás de AgorIA"). This system uses SALUHACKERS. Confirm which is canonical.

## 2 · Brand territory

**Central theme.** *"No enseñamos IA. Enseñamos a los sanitarios a quitarse las partes más aburridas de su trabajo para re-ilusionarse con la profesión."* The technology and method are the means — the end is recovering time, energy and the desire to practice.

**Mother idea.** The clinician has two silent enemies: **bureaucracy** and the **fear of being left behind**. The brand fights both at once — it doesn't add another layer, it *replaces* several with concrete systems that work without being a programmer.

### Six brand values
1.  **Relevancia clínica radical** — if it doesn't answer a real clinical problem, it isn't made.
2.  **Práctica antes que teoría** — applicable before your shift ends.
3.  **Honestidad de producto** — if a tool adds no real clinical value, we don't teach it; we state the sí and the no on patient-data privacy.
4.  **Filtro del FOMO** — only what truly changes your clinical day gets through.
5.  **Comunidad sobre consumo** — not selling courses; a community of clinicians "quemados y asustados por igual, pero con ganas de reilusionarse."
6.  **Respeto por el tiempo** — short modules, actionable, **sin relleno**.

### Six thematic territories
1.  **Tiempo clínico recuperado** — measurable reductions in documentation/admin load.
2.  **El filtro del FOMO** — honest comparisons and criteria on which tools matter.
3.  **El sanitario más allá del sistema** — careers at the health × tech intersection.
4.  **Construir con IA sin saber programar** — custom GPTs, no-code, vibecoding.
5.  **Detrás de SALUHACKERS** — radical transparency on building the school.
6.  **IA que ayuda, no que asusta** — real cases, real metrics, and the limits.

### What it is NOT
Not an AI/health news channel · no generic promises ("domina la IA") · doesn't debate health *politics* (works on what the professional can change) · never teaches untested tools (a founder must have used it in a real context) · doesn't compete on price, competes on *it works and has clinical judgment behind it* · doesn't impose one path.

## 3 · Content fundamentals (voice)

**Language:** Spanish (Spain). Clinical-but-warm. Tech loanwords fine (*workflow, prompt, FOMO*).

**Stance — "nosotros, sanitarios."** Speaks **as a fellow health professional**, first-person plural ("**nuestra** profesión"), addressing the reader as **tú**. A peer who has been in the trenches, never a tech vendor talking down.

**Emotional register:** earnest, motivational, a little defiant. Names the real pain (burnout, bureaucracy, fear) and answers with empowerment. Recurring anchors: **Re-enamorarse de la profesión** · **Dar más tiempo a lo humano** · **Venga lo que venga, estaremos preparados**.

**Casing:** big statements and the wordmark are **ALL CAPS**. Body copy sentence case. Mono kickers uppercase with wide tracking (`> NIVEL: PRO`).

### Tone characteristics
*   **Cercano pero con criterio** — "el colega que sabe más que tú sobre esto pero no te hace sentir ignorante."
*   **Directo, sin rodeos formativos** — "Esto te ahorra 30 minutos por guardia" beats "nuestra metodología integral de capacitación."
*   **Concreto, con datos reales** — not "mejoramos tu práctica clínica" but "**reducimos de 25 a 8 minutos el informe de alta**." Always quantify.
*   **Humano sin ser cursi** — guardias, residentes quemados, enfermeras que no dan abasto — not "usuarios finales en entornos asistenciales."
*   **Valiente** — "nos mojamos": say which tools are worth it and which aren't.
*   **Optimista sin negar la realidad** — from "esto puedes hacerlo," never "aprende o te quedas atrás."

### Rhetorical moves
*   **Contrast frame:** "No enseñamos IA. Enseñamos a…"
*   **GPS metaphor:** "Como un buen GPS no te explica la ingeniería de satélites, sino que te lleva a donde necesitas ir."
*   **Concrete use-cases over hype** — every claim tied to a clinical task with a measurable payoff.

**Emoji:** default none in UI. A single 💡 may flag a value-prop callout in long-form copy only. Prefer mono glyphs.

#### ✓ Do
*   "Reducimos de 25 a 8 minutos el tiempo de informe de alta."
*   "Esto te ahorra 30 minutos por guardia. Sin ser informático."

#### ✕ Don't
*   "Transforma tu práctica clínica"
*   "Domina la IA"
*   "Prepara tu futuro digital."

## 4 · Visual foundations

Aesthetic: **vintage sci-fi pulp poster × hacker terminal, grounded in the clinic.** Warm, tactile, a little rebellious — *not* the cold blue-purple-gradient AI-startup cliché.

### Colour rules

*   **paper:** `#FFFFFF`
*   **ink-900:** `#14201A`
*   **teal-700:** `#234E50`
*   **green-700:** `#2E7D4F`
*   **green-term:** `#34D17E`
*   **rust-500:** `#C2683E`
*   **purple-500:** `#7558B4`
*   **danger:** `#B23A33`

*   **Backgrounds are pure white** (`#FFFFFF`). Warmth comes from ink, accents and grain — never a tinted canvas. A faint neutral (`#F2F1EC`) only for sunken wells.
*   **Ink** `#14201A` — near-black with a green-charcoal cast (the poster lettering).
*   **Deep teal** `#234E50` — primary brand colour; clinical, cool, calm.
*   **Terminal/sage green** `#2E7D4F`, bright `#34D17E` — the "hacker" signal.
*   **Terracotta/rust** `#C2683E` — warm energy accent, primary CTAs, the human counterweight to the teal.
*   **Semantic:** success = green · warning = rust · danger = `#B23A33` · info = teal. **Red is reserved for danger/wrong/deprecated — never a course level.**
*   **Level accents:** iniciados = green `#3FAE6A` · amateurs = teal `#357072` · **pros = violet `#7558B4`**.

### The signature gradient

**45° headline gradient, left→right, pastel teal → green.** All large display headlines are gradient-styled.

*   On white: `linear-gradient(45deg, #357072 0%, #234E50 38%, #2E7D4F 100%)` → `.sh-grad`
*   On dark: `linear-gradient(45deg, #6FA0A0 0%, #357072 45%, #3FAE6A 100%)` → `.sh-grad-bright`
*   **Bright greens (`#34D17E`, `#5BF6A6`) are reserved for CTAs and highlights — never headlines.**
*   No glossy gradients, no glassmorphism, no blue→purple AI gradients.

### Type

| Role | Family | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Anton** | Ultra-bold condensed all-caps; the wordmark and big headlines |
| Condensed headings | **Oswald** | Uppercase headings, labels, buttons |
| Body / UI | **Barlow** | Utilitarian humanist grotesque; all reading text |
| Mono / terminal | **Space Mono** | Kickers, code, `>` prompts, data, CRT motif |

Big headlines: condensed ALL CAPS, tight tracking (`-0.01em`), line-height ≥ 1.0 (Anton glyphs overflow below 1.0). Mono kickers: uppercase, `0.22em` tracking.

### Texture & backgrounds
*   **Grain/halftone** is the signature texture layer — faint printed speckle on white (`multiply`, ~6–10%), stronger on dark/hero surfaces (`overlay`, ~13%). Never a dead-flat hero fill.
*   **Dark "terminal" surfaces** (ink or deep-teal panels, faint scanlines, green mono text) are the second background mode — for code/AI/"hacker" moments.

### Imagery
*   **"Star Wars-style" realistic-epic artwork of real healthcare settings** — a guardia, a screen full of results, hands on a keyboard. **Never stock photos of smiling doctors.**
*   Authenticity over polish: residentes quemados, enfermeras que no dan abasto.
*   Use placeholders to be filled with the brand's real artwork; do not hand-draw SVG scenes.

### Shape language
*   **Radii small and mechanical:** 2 / 4 / 6 / 10px; pills only for tags and levels. Avoid 16px+ pillowy corners.
*   **Borders are assertive hairlines** (1.5px), often ink. Framed/stamped elements 2.5px.
*   **Cards:** white fill, 1.5px border, small radius, low warm ink-tinted shadow. Poster-like elements use the **hard offset stamp shadow** `3px 3px 0 #14201A`.
*   **Glow** reserved for active terminal/AI elements on dark surfaces.

### Motion
Restrained and mechanical. 120–200ms, `ease-out`/`ease`. Things slide and snap — they don't bounce or float. The only personality motion is the **terminal cursor blink** and occasional CRT scanline shimmer. No parallax, no spring easing, no decorative loops.

### Interaction states
*   **Hover:** darken fills one step (rust-500 → rust-700); ghost elements get a faint tint; links shift to terminal green.
*   **Press:** translate ~1px down/right and reduce the stamp shadow ("stamped down"); `scale(0.98)` acceptable on icon buttons.
*   **Focus:** 2px terminal-green outline, 2px offset.
*   **Disabled:** drop to faint ink, no shadow, `not-allowed`.

### Layout
*   **Generous white space is a brand principle** — "dejar respirar el diseño como se deja respirar al profesional." Explicitly avoid the **overloaded medical-congress PowerPoint / feature-catalog** look.
*   Grid/flex with `gap`. Standard rhythm: **mono kicker → big condensed headline → body**. Heroes and section headers may go full-bleed with imagery or dark terminal panels.

## 5 · Iconography
No proprietary icon set exists in the provided materials.

*   **Icon set: Lucide** — outline, ~2px stroke, square-ish geometry; matches the hairline-border language and reads on white and dark. **⚠ Substitution — replace if a brand set exists.**
*   **Sizing:** 20–24px default, `stroke-width: 2`, `currentColor` so icons inherit ink/teal/green. Never mix filled and outline styles.
*   **Mono glyphs as icons:** the terminal motif uses typographic glyphs — `>` `_` `//` `[ ]` `›` `▸` `■` — in Space Mono, for kickers, bullets and terminal chrome.
*   **Emoji:** avoid in UI.
*   **Healthcare motifs** (ECG line, heart, clock, scrubs) belong to *illustrative imagery*, not the UI icon system. For UI use Lucide `activity`, `heart-pulse`, `clock`, `stethoscope`.
*   **Never hand-draw bespoke SVG icons.**

## 7 · Component patterns

### Buttons
Oswald 600, uppercase, `.06em` tracking, `--r-sm`, 1.5px border. Press: `translateY(1px)`.

*   **primary** — rust fill `#C2683E`, border `#A8552E` → hover `#A8552E`
*   **brand** — teal fill `#234E50`, border `#16383A` → hover `#16383A`
*   **ghost** — transparent, ink border → hover `rgba(20,32,26,.06)`
*   **term** — ink fill + bright green mono label, no uppercase → hover adds `--glow-term`
*   **disabled** — faint ink, no shadow

### Level badges
Mono 700, 11px, `.1em` tracking, pill, 7px dot + label. iniciados green-100/green-900 · amateurs teal-100/teal-900 · **pros purple-100/purple-900**. On dark: `rgba(0,0,0,.28)` bg, white text.

### Course card
White fill, 1.5px ink border, `--r-md`, **stamp shadow** `3px 3px 0` (grows to `5px 5px 0` + `translate(-2px,-2px)` on hover), teal header strip carrying a `> CURSO 03` mono kicker and the level badge; body has Oswald title (line-height ≥ 1.2), Barlow description, meta tag + rust "Empezar →" CTA.

### Form fields
Barlow 15px, white fill, 1.5px `--border-strong`, `--r-sm`; Oswald uppercase 12px label; focus = ink border + 2px green-term outline at 2px offset.

### Terminal panel
Ink `#14201A` bg, teal-900 border, three rust/olive/green dots in a header bar, Space Mono body with `academia@saluhackers:~$` prompt, bright-green output, blinking `_` cursor.

### Tags
Mono 11px, faint border, `--r-sm`, optional 13px Lucide icon.

## 8 · Deck / presentation rules

1920×1080. Two slide modes only: **white** (default, generous whitespace) and **dark terminal** (impact — covers, dividers, quotes, closings). Max these two backgrounds per deck.

*   Display headlines gradient-styled (`.sh-grad` on white, `.sh-grad-bright` on dark).
*   Mono kicker (26px, `.22em`) above every headline.
*   Deck text floor 24px; body 32–44px; big data numbers 200px.
*   Footer: mono 20px, hairline rule, brand name left / slide number right.
*   Slide types built: cover · statement · agenda · mother-idea (two enemies) · big-data · comparison · thematic divider with image slot · big quote · closing CTA.
*   Bright green only for CTA chips and highlights, never headline fills.
